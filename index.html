Questions

Good to know

Is this official HEC material?

No. Nothing here is official HEC material. The content is built from student notes and from publicly available questions and examples. The site is made by an HEC Lausanne student, isn't affiliated with HEC Lausanne or UNIL, and doesn't replace your lectures.

How much does it cost?

Which languages are available?

The site works in English and French. Courses are written in English first, and French versions are added course by course.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Log in from any phone, tablet or computer. Progress bars are saved separately on each device.
