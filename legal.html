2.6 How long we keep data

  • Account, progress and usage statistics: as long as your account exists.
  • After you delete your account: sign-in is blocked at once, and everything is erased for good 7 days later. We keep it for these 7 days to undo deletions made by mistake and to handle payment disputes, chargebacks or misuse.
  • Payment records (date, amount, Stripe reference): 10 years, as Swiss accounting law requires (Art. 958f of the Code of Obligations). When your account is deleted, these records are no longer linked to your name or email.
  • Emails: as long as needed to deal with your request.
  • Server logs at our providers: for the short periods those providers set.
